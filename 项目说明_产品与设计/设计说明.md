# Design direction

## Visual read

面向客户和企业人员的生鲜配送业务工具，采用银犁官网的品牌蓝和橙色强调色。页面重视快速理解、结果可信和业务沟通，不把算法术语作为主要视觉内容。

## Dials

- DESIGN_VARIANCE: 5
- MOTION_INTENSITY: 2
- VISUAL_DENSITY: 4

## Tokens

- Paper: `#f5f7fb`
- Surface: `#ffffff`
- Brand blue: `#1750df`
- Brand dark: `#103c9e`
- Action orange: `#ff8133`
- Text: `#1d2a3a`
- Text soft: `#415166`
- Muted: `#718096`
- Border: `#dfe7f1`
- Radius: 10px to 12px, with pill shapes reserved for status tags.

## Composition

- Use left-aligned page titles and a wide content container.
- Use a blue service summary panel with orange actions.
- Prefer one clear conclusion and one useful chart over many technical cards.
- Keep customer-facing labels short, with units shown beside every value.
- Use a light neutral map and blue/orange route distinction for easy reading.

## Motion and states

No continuous animation. Native Streamlit loading states are retained. Reduced-motion CSS disables transitions. Missing and invalid data are presented inline with recovery guidance.

## Integrity rules

Metrics are shown with units and source notes. A route is labeled as a saved result or a quick plan, not a live optimization run. Carbon emissions remain unavailable unless a reliable emission factor exists in the data.
